Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At WhoLikedThat, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and process information when you play our multiplayer trivia game.

1. Information We Collect

Through official integration with TikTok's authentication system (Login Kit) and Data Portability API, our application strictly accesses:

  • Basic TikTok profile information necessary for lobby identification and session management.
  • Authorized historical interaction logs (liked videos, favorites, and shares) strictly required to generate trivia questions.

2. Use of Information

Retrieved data is used solely to power the core game loop: building real-time guessing rounds for players in private lobbies. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with external third parties.

3. Storage and Security

Your data is processed securely. Local caching in SQLite is limited to temporary interaction records required during active game sessions to prevent unnecessary API overhead.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy or data processing, feel free to open an issue on our official GitHub Repository.
