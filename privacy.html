Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At WhoLikedThat, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Through the official integration with TikTok's authentication system (Login Kit) and the Data Portability API, our application accesses strictly:

  • Basic TikTok profile information necessary for authentication.
  • Authorized historical activity logs (such as liked videos, favorites, or shares) exclusively for personal viewing and analysis within your user dashboard.

2. Use of Information

The retrieved data is used solely to provide the core functionality of the application: displaying an organized summary of your TikTok interaction history. We do not share, sell, or disclose any personal data to third parties.

3. Storage and Security

Your data is processed securely. The application limits local data storage to what is strictly necessary for session management and recent user queries.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, you can reach out via the official project repository on GitHub.
